## Notes
The Sprint 2 planning meeting and planning poker took place after some Sprint 2 development tasks had already been started.

This was due to scheduling issues for the planning meeting. As a result, development began on some of the sprint 2 backlog items before we had formally completed sprint planning and planning poker.

During the planning meeting, we reviewed the sprint 2 backlog and carried out planning poker to estimate the relative size and complexity of the selected development tasks.

We identified the late sprint planning meetings as an issue with the sprint 2 process. For future sprints, we agreed that sprint panning and planning poker should be scheduled and completed before development work for the sprint begins.
